## ⚡ Global UX & Performance Rules for Shift 80
1. **Perceived Performance:** Every click must produce immediate visual feedback (< 50ms) using optimistic UI and active tap scales.
2. **Typography & Spacing:** Standardized clean fonts (Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans) with crisp hierarchy and no cluttered text walls.
3. **Palette Harmony:** Warm, welcoming hospitality color accents (Amber-500, Emerald-500, Rose-500, Indigo-500) paired with clean slate neutrals.
